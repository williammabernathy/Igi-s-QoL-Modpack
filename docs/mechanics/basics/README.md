# Basics

This stage covers the systems you meet from the start of a new game: how a shot turns into damage, how enemies, gear and loot scale with level, what the parts of a combat station do, and how boarding works. Some numbers change as you level. Escalation tiers unlock at set captain levels, and loot rarity widens with level. The pages give those levels where they matter.

| [Glossary](glossary.md) | What each term and abbreviation on these pages means, and the exact text the game shows for it. |
| [Combat Damage](combat-damage.md) | How a shot becomes hull damage: stats, damage types, critical hits, shields, armor and resistances. |
| [Scaling](scaling.md) | How enemy HP and damage grow with level, ship rank and Escalation, and what loot rarity to expect at a level. |
