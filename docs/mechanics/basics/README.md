# Basics

Systems you meet from a new game: how a shot turns into damage, how enemies, gear and loot scale with level, what the parts of a combat station do, and how boarding works. Some numbers change as you level (Escalation tiers unlock at set player levels, loot rarity widens with level); the pages give those levels where they matter.

| Page | Answers |
|---|---|
| [Glossary](glossary.md) | What each term and abbreviation on these pages means and the exact text the game shows for it |
| [Combat damage](combat-damage.md) | How a shot becomes hull damage: stats, damage types, crits, shields, armor and resistances |
| [Scaling](scaling.md) | How enemy HP and damage grow with level, ship rank and Escalation, and what loot rarity to expect at a level |
| [Combat stations](stations.md) | What each station part does, how many hit points it has, what it drops, what destroying one costs in reputation, and which parts to kill first |
| [Boarding](boarding.md) | How boarding and facility operations are simulated, and what the numbers on their tooltips mean |

The war between the factions opens at level 28; that is the [Conquest](../conquest/README.md) stage.
